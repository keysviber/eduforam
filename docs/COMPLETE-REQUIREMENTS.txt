EDUCATION FORUM — COMPLETE DEVELOPMENT CHANGES & REQUIREMENTS

Please use this as the complete list of changes and improvements. Nothing from the previous list should be removed.

1. DIRECT CHAT
- Add direct one-to-one chat between users.

2. LANGUAGE CHAT BOARD
- Add a chat board inside the language-learning section.
- Users can send messages in the language they are learning.
- The purpose is to allow users to practise and see whether they understand what other people are saying.

3. USERNAMES
- Introduce usernames for every user.
- Users should be able to create their own username.

4. PROFILE PICTURES & ABOUT ME
Every user should have:
- Profile picture.
- Simple "About Me" section.

5. LEADERBOARD NAMES
- Leaderboards should display people's names/usernames rather than anonymous identifiers.

6. TEACHER CLASSROOM INVITATION LINK
- When a teacher creates a classroom, the teacher should receive a unique invitation link for that classroom.
- The teacher can copy and send the link to students.
- Students click the link to go directly to the classroom/join page.
- Students should not have to manually enter an invitation code.
- The teacher should be able to see which students have joined their classroom.

7. VIDEO WATERMARK
- Add a watermark to videos.

8. SHARE MY TALENT — TIKTOK/REELS-STYLE FEED
- The "Share My Talent" video section should scroll like a simplified TikTok/Reels feed.
- Videos should be displayed vertically.
- Users should swipe up/down to move between videos.
- One video should take up most/all of the screen.
- Videos should automatically play when they become active.
- This applies specifically to the Share My Talent section.
- Do not mix Share My Talent videos with Video Lessons.

9. VIDEO SHARE LINK
- The share button currently does not provide a link.
- Add a proper shareable link when users share a video.

10. VIDEO UPLOAD — SUBJECT SELECTION
- When uploading a video, users should select the subject from a list/dropdown.
- They should not manually type the subject.

11. INTERNATIONAL EDUCATION LEVELS
The education-level system must support different countries and their terminology.

Zimbabwe → UK approximate equivalent:
- Form 1 → Year 8
- Form 2 → Year 9
- Form 3 → Year 10
- Form 4 → Year 11
- Form 5 / Lower 6 → Year 12
- Form 6 / Upper 6 → Year 13

UK → Zimbabwe approximate equivalent:
- Year 8 → Form 1
- Year 9 → Form 2
- Year 10 → Form 3
- Year 11 → Form 4
- Year 12 → Form 5 / Lower 6
- Year 13 → Form 6 / Upper 6

The exact equivalence can vary between education systems, so the system should be designed to support additional countries rather than being built only around Zimbabwe/UK terminology.

Example:
A Zimbabwean student can select:
Country: Zimbabwe
Education level: Form 5

A UK student can select:
Country: United Kingdom
Education level: Year 12

The system should understand that these are broadly equivalent levels when determining quiz groups, learning content and other age/education-based features.

12. CHANGE VOICE — LANGUAGE LEARNING
- We want to change the current metallic/default language-learning voice.
- We want to know whether we can provide our own human voice for the system to use.
- Ask the developer whether we can provide recorded voice/audio and have the app use it for pronunciation.

If possible, clarify what needs to be provided, such as:
- High-quality voice recordings.
- A specific script containing all words/sentences that need to be recorded.
- Separate recordings for individual words/sentences if required.
- WAV/MP3 or another required format.
- Required recording quality/sample rate.
- If an AI/custom voice model is required, tell us exactly what recordings are needed.

The voice must only be used with the appropriate permission from the voice owner.

13. SAFE ROOM APPLICATION
- Users should be able to apply to become a reply/moderator of a Safe Room.
- They should submit a video explaining why they believe they qualify.
- The application should be reviewed before approval.

14. VIEW PASSWORD
- Add a show/hide password option when entering passwords.

15. QUIZ SYSTEM — ADMIN QUESTION BANK
- Admins should be able to create and manage quiz questions from the admin dashboard.
- Admins should be able to:
  - Create questions.
  - Add answer options.
  - Select the correct answer.
  - Assign points.
  - Assign questions to the appropriate age/form group.
  - Edit questions.
  - Delete questions.
  - Continuously add new questions.

Users should NOT all receive the exact same questions.

16. QUIZ — THREE-GRADE GROUPS
- Users should be divided into groups containing 3 educational grades/levels.
- Each group should have its own question pool.

Example:

Group A:
- Year/Form 7
- Year/Form 8
- Year/Form 9

Group B:
- Year/Form 10
- Year/Form 11
- Year/Form 12

The exact grouping should be configurable.

Users within the same 3-grade group can receive questions from the same question pool.

However, users should not necessarily receive the exact same questions at the same time. Questions should be randomly selected from the appropriate group's question bank.

17. QUIZ — 30-SECOND TIMER
- Add a 30-second timer to the quiz.
- If the 30 seconds runs out, the question is automatically marked as wrong.

18. QUIZ — TWO CATEGORIES

CATEGORY 1 — SPEED QUIZ
- Use sets of 10 questions.
- Questions should generally be simple/quick questions.
- Example: 1 + 3.
- Record the total time taken to complete the 10-question set.
- The leaderboard should show the shortest completion times.

CATEGORY 2 — HARD QUESTIONS
- Harder questions.
- Users earn points for correct answers.
- Wrong answers remove points.
- Leaderboard resets at the end of every month.
- Monthly rewards:
  - 1st place — $15
  - 2nd place — $10
  - 3rd place — $5

19. QUIZ — 10-QUESTION SETS
- Questions should be organised into sets of 10.
- After completing all 10 questions, calculate the user's total score.
- Transfer the result to the overall leaderboard.
- Getting a question wrong removes 3 points while completing the set.

20. QUIZ — ANSWER COLOURS
- If the user selects the wrong answer, the answer they selected turns RED.
- The correct answer turns GREEN.

21. QUIZ — SOUND & MUSIC
- Use the same constant background beat/music during the quiz.
- Add a sound effect when a user gets an answer correct.
- Add a different sound effect when a user gets an answer wrong.

22. CLASSROOM — STUDENT VIEW
- Users signed in as Students should NOT see the "Create Classroom" option.
- Only appropriate teacher accounts should have the ability to create classrooms.

23. TEACHER ACCOUNT VERIFICATION
When someone registers as a Teacher:
- Their account should initially go into Pending status.
- They must provide a picture of their ID.
- They must provide a video of themselves teaching the subject they want to teach.
- The teacher account should be reviewed/approved before going live.

Once the teacher is approved:
- Their account goes live.
- Everything they post should go live instantly.
- Their posts should not require individual approval after the teacher has been approved.

24. VIDEO LESSONS SEPARATION
- The Video Lessons section should only show Video Lessons.
- Do not mix Video Lessons with videos uploaded to the Share My Talent/Reels section.

25. LEARN SECTION — VIDEO UPLOADS
- The Learn section currently only allows file attachments.
- Add the ability to upload/attach videos as learning materials.

26. SETTINGS
Add a proper Settings section containing:
- Dark theme.
- Change profile.
- Change grade/education level.

Move "Change Grade" from its current location into Settings.

27. UPLOADED MATERIAL — AUTHOR
- Remove the requirement to manually display/type an author name for uploaded materials.
- Automatically associate uploaded material with the user account that uploaded it.
- The system should know who uploaded the material from their account.

28. NOTIFICATION PERMISSION
- Show a pop-up asking the user to allow notification permissions.
- The purpose is to allow the user to receive app notifications.
- The user should be able to accept or decline the permission.

29. USERNAME SYMBOL
When users create/display a username:
- Use the ☆ symbol instead of a hashtag.
- Example:
  NOT: #Tapiwa123
  USE: ☆Tapiwa123
- The ☆ should be part of the visual username format wherever the username is displayed.

30. POWERED BY XENUS
Add the branding:

Powered by Xenus

Use this wherever the "Powered by" branding is required.

31. INTERNATIONAL SUPPORT
- The app should not be built only around Zimbabwean education terminology.
- Support different countries and their education systems.
- Country selection should be available during registration/profile setup.
- Education levels should adjust appropriately according to the selected country.
- The system should still be able to identify broadly equivalent educational levels for features such as quizzes and learning content.

32. DATE OF BIRTH
- When entering date of birth, use a date picker/calendar.
- Users should select their date rather than manually typing it where possible.

33. COUNTRY SELECTION
- When selecting a country, provide a selectable country list/dropdown.
- Users should not have to manually type their country.

34. INVITATION LINKS
- Use invitation links instead of manually entered invitation codes where invitations are required.
- This particularly applies to teachers inviting students into classrooms.

35. GENERAL SELECTION/DROPDOWN IMPROVEMENT
Where practical, use selectable options/dropdowns rather than making users manually type information.

This applies particularly to:
- Country.
- Date of birth.
- Subject.
- Grade/education level.
- Other predefined categories.

FINAL SUMMARY

The main areas being changed are:

1. Communication — Direct chat + language chat board.
2. Profiles — Usernames, ☆ symbol, profile pictures and About Me.
3. Classrooms — Teacher verification, invitation links and student/teacher permissions.
4. Videos — Watermarks, shareable links, Share My Talent Reels-style feed and separate Video Lessons.
5. Education system — UK, Zimbabwe and future international grade support.
6. Language learning — Customisable voice.
7. Safe Rooms — Application and video verification.
8. Quiz — Admin-controlled question bank, 3-grade groups, randomised questions, timers, points, leaderboards, rewards, sounds and answer colours.
9. Settings — Dark mode, profile changes, grade changes and notifications.
10. Learning materials — Video uploads and automatic account ownership.
11. Branding — Powered by Xenus.
