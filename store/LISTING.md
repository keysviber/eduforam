# Education Forum — Play listing draft

Status: prepared for owner review; not submitted. Use only after the launch catalogue, account flows and classroom operations are verified.

## Listing text

App name: Education Forum

Short description: Explore learning resources, practise languages and join your classroom.

Full description:

Education Forum brings learning resources and beginner language practice together in one place.

Explore the library, search for resources and save books for later. Choose your school grade to see resources for your grade and the grades immediately above and below it on Home. Search the wider catalogue whenever you need other levels.

Start French, Dutch or Spanish with greetings and everyday words. Work through short categories and quizzes, and listen to pronunciation using the voices available on your device. Language practice progress is saved on your current device.

Join a classroom with an invitation code to access resources shared by your administrator. Submit learning materials for review before they become publicly available.

An internet connection is required for accounts, the shared catalogue, classrooms and uploaded resources. Available materials depend on the published catalogue. Pronunciation availability depends on your device's installed voices.

## Release notes — 1.1.0
